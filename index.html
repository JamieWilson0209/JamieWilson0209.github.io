About

I'm a final-year neuroscience undergraduate at the University of Edinburgh. My dissertation project involves building a computational pipeline for calcium imaging analysis of iPSC-derived brain organoids.

The pipeline processes raw fluorescence recordings into quantified neural activity, and I'm using it to compare network dynamics between control and CEP41 R242H mutant organoids.

Outside of my degree, I'm interested in biologically-inspired computation and the connections between neuroscience and physics.
